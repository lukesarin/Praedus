Week by week, with a lesson first

A study set for MATH 120, Test 3 now goes week by week. Each week is split into its real topics. Every topic starts with a short lesson (the idea, then one example solved step by step), then problems with choices A–D. Math is shown properly: $x^2$, $\frac{dy}{dt}$, $\sqrt{100-36}$, $\pi r^2$.

Stuck? Instead of guessing, flip the problem: it shows the full solution, you tap Got it, and it counts as skipped (resets the streak, not a wrong answer). 3 right in a row learns the topic; 2 wrong or skipped brings the lesson back. The three options differ only in how the flipped solution appears. Everything is clickable; each preview has light/dark.

A. Real flip

The problem is a card. “I’m stuck” turns it over to the worked solution.

  • Good: feels most like a flashcard, which is what you described.
  • Trade-off: the problem disappears while you read the solution, so you flip back and forth.

B. Solution opens underneath Recommended

“I’m stuck” opens the solution right below the problem.

  • Good: the problem and its numbers stay on screen while you follow each step, which matters most for math.
  • Good: long solutions just scroll.
  • Trade-off: less of a “card” feel than A.

C. Solution side by side

“I’m stuck” opens the solution in a column next to the problem.

  • Good: problem and solution both fully visible on a laptop.
  • Trade-off: narrow columns for long steps; on phones it stacks like B anyway.
