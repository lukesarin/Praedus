What did we cover that week?

Click any week on the donut to see what was due that week and what it covered: each assignment’s name, its topic from Canvas, the day it was due, and whether it’s done, missing or reviewed.

Same demo semester as before (today is Week 13, MATH 120 with Test 3 picked). Week 10 starts open. Click other weeks, click the same week or press Esc to close, and click an assignment to see where it would open its details.

A. Card beside the weekRecommended

A card opens in the nearest corner of the chart, joined to the week by a thin line.

The card never covers the week you clicked, and the line shows which slice it belongs to.

  • Good: clearly tied to the week you clicked.
  • Good: room for topics and due days without squeezing.
  • Trade-off: covers some other weeks while it’s open.

B. In the center

The hole in the middle turns into the week.

The class name steps aside and the center shows the week’s work. Nothing covers the ring.

  • Good: calm: nothing pops over the chart.
  • Trade-off: small space, so long weeks show the first three and a count.
  • Trade-off: topics get shortened.
praedus, Study map

C. Drawer

A strip slides up from the bottom of the chart.

The week’s work is laid out across the full width, two columns wide. Feels most like the rest of the app on a phone.

  • Good: the most room, and nothing covers the selected week.
  • Good: natural on phones.
  • Trade-off: your eye has to jump from the slice to the bottom edge.
praedus, Study map

D. Peek and pin

Hover a week for a quick list; click to keep the card open.

Same card as A when clicked, plus a small preview while your pointer moves around the ring.

  • Good: fastest way to scan many weeks.
  • Trade-off: hover doesn’t exist on phones, so phones only get the click.
  • Trade-off: more things moving on screen.

In all four the selected week gets an outline, and the panel on the right still works as built. Picking a test closes the week card.
