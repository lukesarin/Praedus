The intro

Your idea, playing: the S draws itself, a short pause, the dot lands like a beat, then it cuts to the app. All three versions share the same opening and differ only in the cut. Each runs about 2.5 seconds, can be skipped with a click or any key, and becomes a simple fade for anyone whose device is set to reduce motion.

1. Hard cut

The dot lands, a beat, then the app is simply there. Crisp and confident, like a film cut.

2. The dot opens the app My pick

After landing, the dot expands into a circle that reveals the app from where it sits. The logo literally becomes the doorway.

3. Fade up

The mark fades away as the app settles in from slightly below. The softest and calmest of the three.

Welcome and log in

Three designs for the first screen. Each has a Log in / Sign up switch and a theme switch. Sign-in is email and password, or Continue with Google. The text is close to final.

My pick

Intro: 2, the dot opens the app. It completes your idea. The S draws, the dot lands, and that dot becomes the doorway into the app. It's memorable without being flashy, and it reads as one continuous motion.

Welcome: B, split with the live study map. It shows what Praedus does before anyone signs up: courses, due-date rings and mint study time drifting gently on the right. It's also the screen most likely to make a student curious enough to create an account.

  • A is the quickest to read and the most familiar, and works best on phones. B collapses to A on narrow screens anyway.
  • C has the most personality, but the big headline needs copy you're happy with long term.
  • Whichever you choose: "Forgot password?" sends a reset email, new accounts confirm their email address, and errors are plain ("That email and password don't match").
