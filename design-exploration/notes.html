Drop your notes in once

Each class gets one place for notes. Drag in slides, study guides or photos of your notes, several at a time, or paste text. Praedus sorts each one into the right week on its own, so a study set for Test 3 automatically uses your notes from weeks 9 to 13.

Try it for real: drag PDFs or photos from your desktop onto any option, or paste with ⌘V. No files handy? Use Drop sample notes on each preview. Weeks that have notes get a small page mark, the week card lists them, and the study set step (pick Test 3, then Make a study set) shows which notes it will use.

A. Drop area in the panelRecommended

A dashed box at the top of the panel, for the class you’re looking at.

Always visible, so it’s obvious that notes go there. Once you’ve added some, it says how many and which weeks they landed in.

  • Good: the easiest to discover: you can’t miss it.
  • Good: also works with Choose files and paste.
  • Trade-off: pushes the tests list down a little.
praedus, Study map

B. Drop anywhere

Drag files over the map and the whole thing becomes a drop area.

A small Notes button in the header lists what you’ve added. A quiet line under the chart says you can drop notes anywhere.

  • Good: the least effort: no target to aim for.
  • Good: keeps the panel just for tests.
  • Trade-off: easier to miss until someone tells you.
praedus, Study map

C. Notes strip

A thin bar under the chart with your latest notes and a dashed Add button.

Your notes stay in sight next to the semester they belong to.

  • Good: notes always visible with the chart.
  • Trade-off: takes height from the donut.
  • Trade-off: only the latest few fit; the rest are a count.

In all three: drop several files at once, paste text with ⌘V, or click to choose files. PDFs (including slides exported as PDF), photos and pasted text work. Notes are saved to the class, not to one test, so they’re ready for every test that covers their week.
