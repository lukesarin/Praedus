Making cards takes about a minute

The first week of a study set takes Claude about 60–90 seconds: it reads your notes, writes a lesson and problems, and checks every math answer. The rest of the weeks are made while you study. These options make that wait clear instead of looking stuck.

Every option shows a live timer and says “about a minute”. Below the options, the confirm step gets one quiet line so it’s expected before you click.

A. The wave draws itself

The Praedus mark animates in a slow loop: the wave draws, lands on the dot.

  • Good: calm and on-brand; the same motion as the sign-in intro.
  • Trade-off: pretty but says little about what’s happening.
praedus, Study set

B. What it’s doing, step by step

Four steps tick off on a schedule, with a thin bar that fills over about a minute.

  • Good: feels like progress, so the wait seems shorter.
  • Trade-off: the steps are timed estimates, not real progress (the app can’t see inside Claude’s work); if it runs long, the last step just keeps spinning.
praedus, Study set

C. What’s coming, while you wait Recommended

Shows what Week 9 covers and which of your notes it’s reading, with the timer up top.

  • Good: honest and useful: you see exactly what the cards will be about, and can leave and come back.
  • Good: nothing pretends to be progress it isn’t.
  • Trade-off: quieter than A or B.
praedus, Study set
